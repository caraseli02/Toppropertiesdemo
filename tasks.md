# Tasks

Ordered implementation queue for the TopProperties Mallorca luxury demo.

## 1. Hero and search entry refinement

Goal: make the top-of-funnel experience unmistakable and premium.

Acceptance criteria:

- Hero copy supports the agreed positioning: “find best options for home in Mallorca”.
- Search entry points are obvious, left-aligned, and mobile-friendly.
- Primary CTA path is clear without relying on placeholders or disabled actions.

## 2. Property card and grid hierarchy

Goal: make the listing surface easier to scan and more editorial.

Acceptance criteria:

- Cards prioritize location, price, and signature selling points.
- The grid reads well at desktop and 375px mobile widths.
- The best listings feel curated rather than repetitive.

## 3. Property detail, gallery, and contact flow

Goal: make the premium detail experience feel polished and trustworthy.

Acceptance criteria:

- Gallery/detail interactions preserve the luxury tone.
- Contact flow has clear trust signals and no dead ends.
- Keyboard and mobile interactions remain usable.

## 4. Infrastructure polish and handoff readiness

Goal: keep the repo easy for the next agent to continue.

Acceptance criteria:

- `vp install`, `vp dev`, `vp check`, `vp test`, and `vp build` all stay documented and working.
- `PROGRESS.md` and `DECISIONS.md` stay current.
- The repo remains easy to pick up without reading prior chat history.
