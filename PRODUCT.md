# PRODUCT.md

## Register

Brand surface. Design IS the product. This is a luxury real estate showcase where the visual experience communicates exclusivity, taste, and trust before a single word is read.

## Audience

High-net-worth individuals browsing luxury properties on mobile between meetings or while traveling. They have limited time, high expectations, and zero tolerance for anything that feels generic or unfinished. They're evaluating the brand as much as the properties.

## Brand Voice

Refined, confident, editorial.

Not shouty luxury. Not quiet minimalism. The tone of a well-edited magazine — each element earns its place. Every pixel says "we curate."

## Visual References

- **Sotheby's International Realty** — authority, restraint, premium photography
- **1stDibs** — editorial curation, rich detail, tactile elegance
- **Monocle magazine** — confident typography, intentional whitespace, worldly sophistication

## Anti-References

- Zillow — utility over feeling, data-dense, generic
- Trulia — template aesthetics, no brand personality
- Any generic real estate template — if it could be any agency's site, it's wrong

## What This Product Is

Top Properties is a luxury real estate demo showcasing curated villas, penthouses, and estates in premium locations worldwide. It's a single-page app (React + Vite + Tailwind v4) with a hero section, property grid with map toggle, filter/search, property detail overlay, and contact modal.

## What Design Must Achieve

1. **Trust in the first viewport** — The brand must feel unmistakable within 2 seconds
2. **Curated, not listed** — Properties should feel hand-selected, not dumped from a database
3. **Mobile-first confidence** — The primary audience is on phones; desktop is secondary
4. **No dead ends** — Every interaction must feel complete. No "coming soon," no placeholders
5. **Emotional arc** — The journey from browsing → exploring → contacting should build desire at every step
